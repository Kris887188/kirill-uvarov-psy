Privacy Policy for Last Light

Effective date: 20 September 2026
App: Last Light
Package: com.lastlight.mobile
Developer: LAST LIGHT Studio (Kirill Uvarov)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Last Light handles information. The current version of the game is designed to work without user accounts, advertising, analytics, social sign-in, or developer-operated online services.

1. Personal data collected by the game

The current version of Last Light does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier.

2. Game progress and local data

Game progress, settings, and related gameplay state are stored locally on your device so the game can save and restore your progress. This information is not transmitted to LAST LIGHT Studio by the game.

You can remove locally stored app data through Android system settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to any device or Google backup features that you have independently enabled.

3. Google Play and platform services

Last Light is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information independently for app delivery, licensing, security, integrity checks, updates, crash or performance reporting, and other Google Play platform functions. That processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies, not by this policy.

4. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of Last Light does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, developer-operated analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

5. Accounts and purchases

The current version of Last Light does not require or provide a Last Light user account. It also does not currently process payments or in-app purchases through developer-operated systems.

6. Children's privacy

Last Light is not designed specifically for children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the app.

7. Support messages

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, the developer will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your support request. This information is used only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, maintain necessary records, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because the game does not transmit gameplay or personal data to LAST LIGHT Studio, the developer normally has no in-game personal data to retain or delete.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, troubleshoot, maintain essential records, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the developer, unless retention is required by law.

9. Security

Last Light minimizes privacy risk by keeping game progress on the device and by not operating a developer database of player accounts or gameplay profiles in the current version. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the developer aims to collect no more information than is necessary.

10. Future changes

If future versions introduce features such as advertising, analytics, cloud saves, accounts, online services, or other data processing, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated to reflect those changes.
